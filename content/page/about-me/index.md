# Hi I'm Sin Yin!

**A Media and Communication Graduate**, majoring in *Advertising* and *Social Media* with a minor in *Digital Marketing*, from INTI International College Subang, in collaboration with Swinburne University of Technology.

I’m naturally curious and analytical, especially when it comes to the technical and data-driven side of marketing. 

During my internship at PwC Malaysia, I got hands-on with CRM campaigns in Salesforce, website and SEO work in Adobe Experience Manager, and running my first LinkedIn ads campaign. Before that, I was part of the INTI Sales Support Team, helping with recruitment events and outreach. Together, these gave me a cross-functional understanding of marketing, sales and event operations.

In my free time, I’m keen to upskill, most recently in digital marketing through Google, Mailchimp and Meta courses.

Looking for a **Entry-Level Digital Marketing Role**, feel free to reach out!
